Initially, I had incorporated a button into my breadboard. However, I decided to remove the button. At first, I was thinking of creating a mechanism that allows you to touch the flower and have it open, so I tried to incorporate a copper touchpad on the petal. However, aesthetically, this did not match my vision and was also a little harder to operate. So I decided to switch it out and instead change the code so that the flower is now controlled through my phone over Wi-Fi by connecting to the ESP32’s IP address.
